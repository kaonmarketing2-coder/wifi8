Why Wi-Fi 8 Changes Everything

Built on IEEE 802.11bn (Ultra High Reliability), Wi-Fi 8 introduces coordinated multi-AP intelligence, expanded spectrum, and next-level QoS — delivering enterprise-grade performance to every environment.

88Gbps
Ultra High Throughput
Nearly double Wi-Fi 7's maximum throughput with mmWave integration and 8 spatial streams, enabling seamless 8K streaming and high-density deployments.
Multi-AP
Multi-AP Coordination
Industry-first standardized cooperative scheduling across multiple access points, eliminating inter-AP interference and enabling seamless roaming across entire properties.
UHR
Ultra High Reliability
Advanced LDPC codes, Enhanced In-Device Coexistence (IDC), and TXOP Preemption deliver mission-critical reliability for industrial IoT, healthcare, and enterprise environments.
2027
Coming to Market
WFA certification planned for late 2027. Operator solutions targeted for mid/late 2027, with smartphones and PCs following in Q3 2027 — a faster transition than any previous Wi-Fi generation.
DSO
Dynamic Sub-Channel Operation
NPCA (Non-Primary Channel Access) and Dynamic Sub-Channel Operation enable intelligent spectrum utilization, maximizing throughput in congested RF environments.
dRU
Distributed Resource Units
New dRU (Distributed Resource Unit) allocation enables more efficient OFDMA scheduling with multiple resource units per station, enhancing capacity in high-density scenarios.

Feature Breakdown

New Capabilities in Wi-Fi 8

Wi-Fi 8 introduces a comprehensive set of innovations targeting network reliability, quality of service, throughput, and range — all within the same cost-efficient silicon.

Network
Inter-AP Coordination
Standardized cooperation between access points for interference cancellation and joint scheduling across the entire Wi-Fi network.
Reliability
Advanced LDPC Codes
Next-generation error correction codes reduce packet loss in challenging RF conditions, ensuring robust links even at the edge of coverage.
QoS
Improved EDCA & HIP
High-Priority Enhanced Distributed Channel Access enables deterministic latency for time-sensitive applications like XR, VoIP, and industrial control.
Throughput
New MCS Rates
Additional Modulation & Coding Scheme entries including Unequal Modulation and 8192-QAM (under consideration) push spectral efficiency to new limits.
Spectrum
Non-Primary Channel Access
NPCA allows secondary channel utilization without interfering with primary operations, extracting more capacity from licensed spectrum.
Mobility
Seamless Roaming
Coordinated handoff between access points eliminates roaming disruption, supporting uninterrupted 4K/8K streaming and real-time conferencing on the move.
Coexistence
Enhanced IDC
In-Device Coexistence improvements minimize self-interference between Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular radios co-located in smartphones and IoT devices.
Resource Mgmt
Distributed Resource Units
dRU enables flexible OFDMA scheduling with distributed sub-carrier allocation, improving fairness and capacity in dense multi-client environments.
